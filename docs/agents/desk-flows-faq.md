## What does "promote" do?

**Promote…** marks a lesson as handled, with an optional note. The lesson leaves the candidates and shows under **Promoted lessons** with its time, note, and GitHub issue. For the full behavior, read "Lessons" and "The desk UI" in `docs/agents/agent-desk.md`.
