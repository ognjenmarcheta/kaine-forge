Follow the Byteforge website's new red-orange accent. Brand and selected fills are `#d4421a` in both themes, and text on them is white through `--ds-text-brand-inverse`. Hovered and pressed fills get darker so white text stays WCAG AA. Links, brand text, selected tints, focus rings, and brand borders and icons move to matching values. Select action items now use `--ds-text-brand` instead of the fill color, so they stay readable on the dark overlay.
