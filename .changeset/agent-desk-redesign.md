Redesign the agent desk around the owner's turn. The board opens on Your turn with quick and batch approve for low-risk plans, the issue page has a short header, decision gates with plan risk and file review, a trace of every step, and a stage side sheet, and the shell adds a command palette, shortcuts, an 8-second undo window, and opt-in notifications. Health shows setup progress, Runs adds today's summary, seven-day cards, and cost by role, and lessons can be promoted, unpromoted, or turned into a GitHub issue after the owner confirms the exact text. The engine records plan risk, pause and resume, approve notes, and a seven-day series, and the design system adds the tokens the desk uses.
